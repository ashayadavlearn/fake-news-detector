The pipeline

Four steps, one verdict.

01

Preprocess

Text is lowercased, stripped of punctuation and noise, and lemmatized — the same cleanup the model was trained on.

02

Vectorize

A TF-IDF vectorizer turns the cleaned text into a weighted vector of the words and phrases that matter most.

03

Classify

The best-performing model — chosen automatically from four candidates during training — scores the vector.

04

Report

You get a REAL / FAKE verdict with a confidence percentage, logged to a searchable history.
